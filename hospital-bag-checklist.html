Hospital bag checklist

Tick off what you pack for you, your baby and your birth partner.

Packing early takes the stress out of the last weeks of pregnancy. Many mums aim to have their bag ready by around week 34 to 36. Tick items off as you pack: your progress is saved on this device.

Every hospital is different. Some provide items or give you their own list at an antenatal visit. Please follow your clinic's list where it differs from this one. This is general advice, not medical advice.
