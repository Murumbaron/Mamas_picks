Privacy policy

What we collect, what we do not, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you use Mama Picks Kenya. We try to collect as little as possible.

No accounts, no sign-up

You do not need an account to use this site, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or email address while you browse.

What is stored on your device

When you tap a heart, close an announcement, tick items on a checklist or choose a language, we save that choice in your browser's local storage so it is still there next time. It stays on your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Anonymous counts

When you tap a heart, share a product or use a "Check it out" button, we may record an anonymous count against that product, such as "this product was clicked". These counts are not linked to your name or identity. We use them to see which products are useful.

Analytics

We may use a website analytics service (such as Google Analytics or Plausible) to understand which pages are visited and how people find the site. These services may use cookies or similar technology and receive information such as your approximate location, device and browser.

Advertising and affiliate links

This site may show adverts served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com.

Many product links go to Jumia and are affiliate links. When you click one, Jumia and its partners may set cookies so a purchase can be credited to us. Once you leave our site, Jumia's own privacy policy applies.

Other websites

We link to other websites, including Jumia and book shops. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We aim to respect the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya. If you have emailed us, you may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

Changes to this page

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
