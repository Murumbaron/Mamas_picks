About Mama Picks Kenya

Handpicked pregnancy and baby finds, with free guides and tools for mums in Kenya.

What this site is

Mama Picks Kenya is an independent website that gathers pregnancy, newborn and mum-care products from Jumia Kenya in one place, so they are easier to browse. We also publish free guides and tools for mums in Kenya.

How we choose products

We pick items we think are useful for mums in Kenya and available on Jumia. We only use the "Mum tested" label on a product when someone has actually used it. Everything else is a pick, not a test result.

How the site is paid for

Many links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission from Jumia at no extra cost to you. This helps keep the guides and tools free. We may also show adverts. Neither changes the price you pay.

Prices and availability

Prices, photos, stock and delivery are managed by Jumia and can change at any time. Always check the product page before you order, and read the seller's return policy.

Health information

Our guides and tools are general information to help you shop and plan. They are not medical advice. Please talk to your midwife, doctor or clinic about anything to do with your health or your baby's.
