Baby shower gift ideas for every budget

Useful, loved gifts from small to big, plus ideas for the new mum herself.

Not sure what to bring to a baby shower? The best gifts are the ones parents will actually use. Here are ideas by budget, a few tips, and gifts for mum too.

Quick tips before you buy

  • Ask the parents-to-be what they still need, or whether they have a list.
  • Practical beats cute. Nappies and wipes are never wasted.
  • Choose the 3 to 6 months size for clothes. Newborn sizes are outgrown in weeks.
  • Keep the product link, and check the return policy in case they need to exchange it.

Small gifts

Thoughtful and easy on the pocket.

  • Packs of nappies and baby wipes
  • Soft socks, mittens and hats
  • Muslin cloths or burp cloths
  • A gentle baby soap or lotion set (go for unscented or lightly scented)

Medium gifts

Everyday items parents reach for again and again.

  • A set of swaddle blankets
  • Clothing sets in 3 to 6 months
  • A foldable baby bath
  • A starter set of feeding bottles

Big gifts and group presents

Pool money with friends or family for these.

  • A baby carrier or wrap
  • A baby monitor
  • Cot bedding or a Moses basket
  • A breast pump (ask first, as not every mum wants one)

Gifts for the new mum

  • A maternity pillow for better sleep
  • A rich body butter or belly cream
  • Comfortable nursing bras or a soft robe
  • A gentle postpartum belly wrap (ask first, as some mums prefer not to use one)

What to skip

  • Lots of newborn-size outfits
  • Strongly scented products, which can irritate delicate skin
  • Second-hand car seats, which can have hidden damage
  • Toys with small parts. Check the age label on every toy
